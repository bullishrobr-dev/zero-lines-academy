Pro Tips

Demo on the hand — always

The visual of dead skin rolling off is your strongest proof. Never skip the hand demo.

“Roll it, don’t rub it”

This phrase is crucial. If they rub too hard, it won’t work. Gentle rolling creates the pilling effect.

Mention dermatologists

Say it out loud: doctors and dermatologists send people to this. The driest, the most sensitive, people prone to eczema and psoriasis. Nothing you can say about glow lands as hard as the fact that it is what the worst skin in the room uses.

A scrub is not this

A scrub scratches the dead skin off. This dissolves the glue holding it on and the skin lets go by itself. That is why it is once a week and not every day, and why the bottle lasts a year — you are not sanding your face, you are telling it to let go.

“Does it hurt?”

“Hurt? You will feel it working — a bit of warmth, a bit of a tingle, about ten seconds in. That is it doing its job, not hurting you.” Never say “no” flat. She can feel something; denying it makes you a liar ten seconds later.

Tell her how to use it

Clean skin, Sunday night, five minutes. Roll it, rinse it, then your own cream goes on and it works ten times harder because there is nothing dead in the way. Once a week — more is not better, it is just faster through the bottle.

One bottle = one year

Always emphasize longevity. €100 for a full year of treatments is less than €2 per week. Frame it as an investment.

€200 first, always

Start with the Europe price. The Andorra price feels like a gift after that anchor.

The emotional close is everything

“When was the last time you treated yourself?” Say it with genuine feeling. This line works on every demographic.

Works on everyone

Women, men, young, old — this pitch works on every demographic because it delivers instant visual proof and emotional value.

€50 voucher is your secret weapon

Only use the €50 close at the very end. If you drop it too early, you leave money on the table.

One voucher, once

You use the voucher once, and on the peeling it lands straight on the floor: €50, single Peeling only, never the combo. There is no second voucher — inventing another one tells her the first was invented too. And unlike the syringe, you do not go and fetch anybody for this number: on the peeling the maths simply stops there.

Bridge it up to the eyes
