Pro Tips

If she asks what happens after the year

Do not bring this up. It is a one-year treatment and that is where your pitch stops. But if she asks: “After the year you carry on if you are happy with it, or you stop. If you stop, the lines come back slowly — same as the gym. You train for a year, you stop going, your muscles do not vanish overnight. They fade.” Honest, and it sells better than a promise she would have caught you on.

Always start with the Europe price

€500 is the anchor. Everything after feels like a bargain. Never mention the minimum first.

One eye only — the other one is your close

I never do the other eye. Sometimes, inside the speech, I say I will do it in order to close. That is what it is for: at €175, before she stands up — “you know what, let me fix your other eye real quick” — and the voucher comes out WHILE you are doing it. Never a freebie.

Engage the partner ALWAYS

Bring him IN first, every time — the joke, the reveal, the close. He is often the real decision-maker. Only if he is genuinely bad energy do you turn and give everything to her.

If they smile, they buy

Keep humor alive. Playful energy opens wallets. If they’re not smiling, they’re not buying.

100% natural — no chemicals

Always mention this early. It removes fear and positions the product as safe and premium.

Go down the ladder one step at a time

Never jump from €300 to €140. Walk down slowly. Each step feels like you’re doing them a favor.

One voucher, once — the manager owns the floor

You use the voucher once, €175 to €140, with the extra 20%. There is no second voucher — inventing another one tells her the first was invented too. Below that you go full market and you CALL A MANAGER to reach €100.

The two promises seal loyalty

“Promise me you’ll use it. Promise me you’ll tell your friends.” This turns buyers into ambassadors.

This is your crown jewel

The syringe pitch is your strongest weapon. It sells on emotion, proof, and trust. Practice it until it’s second nature.

The claim stops at the year

One syringe — 60 applications, once/week, 1 year, and the one rule: no touching 5h. That is the whole promise and it stops there. Nothing you say may make the effect outlive the treatment, and you never volunteer what happens afterwards — if she asks, the answer is in the pro tips.
