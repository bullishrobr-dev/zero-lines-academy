Pro Tips

Make it sensory — let the hands sell it

The feeling is everything. Let them rub, rinse, and feel. Once they feel the softness, the sale is halfway done.

The flip test is your secret weapon

Flipping the butter jar and showing nothing falls out is a visual proof they can’t argue with. Do it every time.

Use Christmas urgency naturally

“Stock runs faster closer to the holidays, and I’d hate you to miss colors/scents you like.” Timing closes gifts.

Keep the math clean

€60 each. €120 for three (Buy 2, Get 1). Simple, round numbers are easier to say yes to.

Buy 2, Get 2 for premium buyers

When the energy is great and they have a Christmas list — this is your volume play. Same €120, extra product.

Buy 1, Get 1 for fast closes

For hesitant buyers who still felt the demo — this turns 'maybe' into 'yes' instantly. €60 feels like nothing after €120.

€30 Scrub is your final push

When nothing else works, the single Scrub at €30 creates a customer today and a bigger sale tomorrow. No gifts, no extras — just the hero product in their hands.

Keep it fun — jokes, smiles, easy energy

This demo should feel like a spa moment, not a sales pitch. If they smile, they buy.

Lines We Do Not Cross

  • Nobody leaves to think about it. The single at €30 is the last rung and it happens here, today — no card, no price written down, no voucher to take away.
  • A scrub is never what follows a syringe. Sell a syringe and your job is the handover: the upseller is a specialist who is only here for a short time. You do not pitch a second product on top of it.
  • Nothing the shop has to honour afterwards. No refunds, no returns, no money back, no promise of future service — the buffer swap is the nail kit’s, and it is the only one.
  • Talk the skin up as much as you like — the driest, the most sensitive, the ones who cannot put anything on themselves. Never reach for a disease to sell a cream.
  • Never explain why it is cheaper here. The Europe price beside the Andorra price does the whole job on its own — the shop’s standing in Andorra is not a selling point we voice, in any wording.
