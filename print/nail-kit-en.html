The 3-Step Demo

Build it, do not rush it

The magic is in the buildup. Steps 1 and 2 create suspense. Step 3 is the WOW.

  1. 1

    Smooth · Grey Side

    Hold her hand gently. Start with the first two steps on one nail. “Most buffers you see remove the top layer of your nail to make it shiny — that’s actually bad. It makes the nails weak and thin. This one’s different. It smooths, shapes, but doesn’t remove anything.” Show the nail — not shiny yet. “See? Nothing dramatic yet — now wait for the last step.”

  2. 2

    Polish · White Side — Prep

    Use the white strip, buff gently. “Feel that? It’s soft, not rough. It’s actually pushing your natural oils up to the surface — that’s what gives the shine and strengthens the nail.”

THE RULE — she does not leave the shop

If she walks out she is never coming back, so treat every exit as a lost sale. Read the ladder as bundles, not a slide: €120 buys three kits and the same €120 buys four, so the money only really moves at €60 and then at €30. The whole kit at €30 is the bottom rung, not a goodbye — warranty included, in her hands today, and she is a customer. Work all of it before anything ends. Never hand her a card, never write the price down, never send her off to have a think with somebody who is not standing here.

Lines We Do Not Cross

  • The buffer is the one promise you may make, and it is real: she brings the old buffer back, she gets a new one, forever, in any of our stores. Nothing else gets promised — no refunds, no returns, no money back, no service after the till.
  • Nobody leaves to think about it. The whole kit at €30 is the last rung and it happens here, today — no card, no price written down, no voucher to take away.
  • A nail kit is never what follows a syringe. Sell a syringe and your job is the handover: the upseller is a specialist who is only here for a short time. You do not pitch a second product on top of it.
  • Nails, oils, shine, dry hands — talk all of it up as much as you like. Never reach for a disease to sell a kit.
  • Never explain why it is cheaper here. The Europe price beside the Andorra price does the whole job on its own — the shop’s standing in Andorra is not a selling point we voice, in any wording.
